§ What FemiCore Actually Is

FemiCore is a once-daily capsule for women's bladder and urinary tract support. The formula pairs botanical extracts — cranberry, bearberry, berberine, and mimosa pudica — with multiple Lactobacillus probiotic strains.

The underlying idea is the urinary microbiome: researchers have increasingly found that the balance of bacteria in the urinary and vaginal environment plays a role in bladder comfort, urgency, and UTI frequency, and that this balance shifts with menopause, aging, antibiotics, and stress. FemiCore's approach is to support the beneficial bacteria while the botanicals address irritation.

It's positioned for occasional urgency, frequency, nighttime bathroom trips, and confidence concerns — not as a treatment for incontinence, UTIs, or pelvic floor dysfunction. Those are medical territory.
