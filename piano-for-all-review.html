§ What Pianoforall Actually Is

It's ten interactive ebooks with video and audio embedded directly in the pages — roughly 24 hours of video and over a thousand audio exercises — that teach piano starting from chords and rhythm instead of sheet music and scales.

That inversion is the whole product. Traditional lessons start you on scales, note-reading, and exercises, and it's usually a year before you can play something a friend would recognize. Most adults quit long before that. Pianoforall starts you on chord shapes and rhythm patterns so you're playing recognizable music in the first few weeks, then layers theory in behind it.

You download the files and keep them. They work offline on Windows, Mac, Android, iOS, tablets and phones. There's no app, no login, no monthly fee, and nothing expires — which puts it in a different category from Flowkey, Simply Piano and Playground Sessions, all of which are subscriptions that stop working when you stop paying.

§ Who It's For (And Who Should Skip It)

A good buy if you…

  • Are an adult beginner who wants to sit down and play songs people recognize
  • Tried traditional lessons as a kid and quit because scales were tedious
  • Want to accompany yourself singing, jam with others, or improvise
  • Prefer owning files outright to renting an app forever
  • Will actually practice 20 minutes a day without anyone making you

Skip it if you…

  • Need to read sheet music fluently — for exams, ensemble playing, or formal study
  • Want to pursue classical piano seriously; you need a teacher, not a course
  • Are buying it for a child — the self-paced format and improvisation focus suit adults
  • Want feedback on your technique, which no self-paced course can give you
  • Have a history of buying courses and not opening them. This one needs you to show up.

§ Quick Questions

Is Pianoforall a scam?

No, and it's the easiest call on this site. It's a substantial course that's been sold through ClickBank for nearly twenty years with an enforced refund policy. The marketing describes what's actually in the box — which, in this market, genuinely distinguishes it.

Will I be able to read sheet music afterward?

Somewhat, but not fluently — and you should take that seriously. Sight-reading is the acknowledged weak spot of the chord-first approach. If reading music is your goal, either pair this with a dedicated sight-reading method or choose a different course.

Do I need a full-size piano?

No. A basic 61-key keyboard is enough to work through the material. Weighted keys are nicer but aren't required to start.

How long before I can play something?

Most people report playing recognizable material within a few weeks at around 20 minutes a day. "Playing well" is a multi-year project, as it is with any instrument — the chord-first method shortens the time to your first song, not the time to competence.

How do refunds work?

Through ClickBank directly, within 60 days of purchase, from your order confirmation email. You don't negotiate with the seller.
