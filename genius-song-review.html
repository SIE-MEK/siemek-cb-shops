§ What The Genius Song Actually Is

Strip away the marketing and here's the product: a downloadable audio track you listen to for about seven minutes a day, ideally with headphones. The audio is engineered around gamma-frequency brainwave entrainment — the idea that rhythmic sound can encourage your brain to sync toward a target frequency.

Gamma waves are real and well-documented in neuroscience; they're associated with focused attention and learning. The program's premise is that modern life — stress, screens, constant interruption — suppresses this activity, and that daily listening can help encourage it.

That's the whole protocol. No pills, no equipment, no subscription. You buy it once, download it, and listen.
