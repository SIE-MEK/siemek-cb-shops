§ What Yu Sleep Actually Is

Yu Sleep is a liquid sleep-support supplement — you take two droppers about 30 minutes before bed. The formula combines roughly ten ingredients including low-dose melatonin, L-theanine, GABA, 5-HTP, red tart cherry extract, lemon balm, magnesium glycinate, and B vitamins.

The most interesting formulation decision is the melatonin dose. Most sleep products dump in 5–10mg, which is a big reason people wake up groggy. Yu Sleep uses a deliberately low dose designed to work with your body's own production curve rather than steamroll it. That's consistent with where the sleep research has actually been heading.

It's positioned for occasional poor sleep, 3 AM wake-ups, and racing-mind bedtimes — not as a treatment for insomnia, sleep apnea, or any medical condition. It isn't, and no supplement is.
