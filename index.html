How I Review

The same four steps, every product.
I

Separate claim from mechanism

Vendors borrow credibility from real science — "NASA-inspired," "clinically studied." I check whether the research is about the finished product or just one ingredient in it. That gap is where most of the hype lives.

II

Read the money terms

Real price versus anchor price, one-time versus autoship, who processes the refund and for how long. A generous, independently-enforced guarantee changes the risk math more than any ingredient claim.

III

Name what it isn't

No supplement treats a condition. No blueprint ships you a machine. Every review states plainly what the product cannot do and when you should see a professional instead of buying anything.

IV

Say who should skip it

Every verdict includes the people it's wrong for — drug interactions, climates, expectations, budgets. A review that can't tell you to walk away isn't a review, it's an ad.
